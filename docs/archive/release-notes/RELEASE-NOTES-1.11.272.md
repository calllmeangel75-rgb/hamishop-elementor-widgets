# 1.11.272 — staging pre-release

Security and correctness fixes from an audit. A wallet top-up can no longer be settled with Cash on Delivery or with the wallet itself: the gateway list while a top-up is being paid, the pay endpoint, the wallet gateway and the credit step all refuse it, and a top-up that reaches a paid status with such a method earns no credit and gets one private order note. With products in the cart the pay step sends that cart to the gateway, not an older pending order left in the session; a pending top-up or pay-link order is paid from its own order-pay screen, or from the pay step once the cart is empty. The pay widgets show an order named in an order-pay URL only with its order key and to its owner. Enabling Telegram two-factor no longer stores a typed phone number as the account's verified number unless Telegram tied that number to the same chat; numbers stored this way before are not migrated. The five-tries limit of the Telegram login is counted atomically. The ticket CSV export neutralises spreadsheet formulas, and the mobile "added to cart" sheet escapes product data and shows the currency symbol. Also: the state, city and unit icons of the checkout address form, and clean-up on deactivation and uninstall. Wallet arithmetic and the ledger are unchanged. Tested on disposable installs and in CI, not with a real bank gateway: verify the money path on staging first. Not approved for production.
